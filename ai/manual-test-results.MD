Test 3: free most:

Objective: test the extremes to inspect the mechanics

Results: It's clear that there isn't a cap on the affect of the hot fuel break and I think there should be. When pulling all the control rods out, with all 4 pumps on, I was able to get radioactivity over 3500, and fuel temp over 3500 degrees. This instantly causes rad to plummet back down, and the pumps then quickly cool the fuel. So apart from considering a cap on the braking affect of hot fuel, I think reactor temp should be quicker to increase when there is a very large gap. Surely 3500 degree fuel would cause a huge amount of steam in real life, and that would negate the affect of the massive hot fuel brake on rad. Eventually, leaving all control rods out did result in reactor temp building up, and therefore steam. But it took at least 3 large swings in rad and fuel temp for this to happen.
